## 0.2.0 (build 20261102.04) · 2026-11-03

The first public beta.

- Scrobbles what you play in the Music app on this Mac to Last.fm, from the menu bar.
- Keeps plays queued while you're offline and sends them once Last.fm can be reached.
- A library window with your scrobble history, loved tracks, Rewind and Friends.
- Checks for updates weekly by default. Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Never in Settings → General → Updates.
